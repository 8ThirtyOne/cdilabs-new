Autoantibodies have long been associated with autoimmune disease and new research is also uncovering connections between viral exposure and autoimmune disease. There’s also a growing body of evidence that autoantibodies are involved in a diverse range of other disease areas such as cancer, neurodegenerative disease, obesity and many others. It is increasingly clear that each individual’s autoantibody repertoire is both highly informative for disease research and incredibly unique. CDI Labs offers products and services that make autoantibody profiling readily accessible to any lab. Offerings include HuProt™ Human Microarray and PhIP-Seq (Phage ImmunoPrecipitation Sequencing) assays, including VirScan®, HuScan®, MouseScan™.

 

Join us Wednesday, September 25, from 1:15 - 2:15 pm, in Building 10, Room 1 as we explore “Tools for Autoantibody Discovery” and learn how seromics is transforming disease research.
