Empowering Discoveries in Oncology Research

CDI Labs Canada and CDI Labs offer unique and empowering research tools and services that are being used to advance the understanding of immunotherapy response and inform therapeutic development.

Chronic viral infections are known to impact cancer patient outcomes. VirScan™ Viral PhIP-Seq antibody profiling* from CDI Labs Canada has been used in research highlighting that CMV-induced T cell senescence is associated with poorer outcomes in patients with advanced NSCLC treated by checkpoint blockade immunotherapy1.

VirScan allows researchers to determine what antibodies were created by an individual’s unique history of viral exposures. PhIP-Seq is the highest-coverage antigen-specific assay yet developed and enables simultaneous epitope-level antibody profiling versus most vertebrate viruses.

The HuProt™ Human Proteome Microarray from CDI Labs has been used to identify autoantibody signatures that have the potential to predict both disease recurrence and immune-related adverse events in melanoma patients treated with checkpoint inhibitor adjuvant immunotherapy2. HuProt was also used to support findings that indicate that immunotherapies that boost both coordinated B and T cell responses against human ovarian cancer - an immunogenic disease that is currently resistant to checkpoint inhibitors - are likely to show increased therapeutic benefit3.

With over 21,000 GST-purified, full-length human proteins and isoform variants, the HuProt Proteome Microarray is revolutionizing cancer research by unveiling new biomarkers and therapeutic targets that could potentially transform patient care in immuno-oncology.

* VirScan is provided exclusively by CDI Labs Canada.
