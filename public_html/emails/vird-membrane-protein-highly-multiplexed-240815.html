Critical Druggable Targets Including Non-odorant GPCRs

The VirD™ Membrane Protein Microarray enables 429 membrane proteins to be assayed in a single experiment. Viral-based expression technology expresses individual proteins embedded in a cell membrane. Purified virions displaying correctly-folded proteins are then printed onto glass to create VirD arrays. The VirD array focuses on non-odorant GPCRs, but also contains other critical druggable targets such as TNF family receptors. Contact us to learn more.

Development and application of a high-content virion display human GPCR array

Guan-Da Syu, Heng Zhu, et. al.

Human G protein-coupled receptors (GPCRs) respond to various ligands and stimuli. However, GPCRs rely on membrane for proper folding, making their biochemical properties difficult to study.
