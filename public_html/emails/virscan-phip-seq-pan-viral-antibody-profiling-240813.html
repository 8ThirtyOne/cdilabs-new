Individual’s Unique History of Viral Exposures

Viral infections as risk factors for disease have long been established, such as the link between HPV and cancer. However, research continues to elucidate new connections between viral exposure and disease, such as the identification of a mechanistic link for the association between multiple-sclerosis (MS) and Epstein-Barr virus1. 

VirScan® PhIP-Seq is a powerful method of multiplexed analysis that combines next-gen proteomics with DNA high-throughput sequencing, and allows researchers to determine what antibodies were created by an individual’s unique history of viral exposures.
